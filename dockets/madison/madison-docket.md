# The Madison Docket

*The questions for the Saturday Board at the Dane County Farmers' Market,
Capitol Square, Madison.*

Each of these is a real decision taken in the Wisconsin State Capitol between
2014 and 2024. On the day, visitors are given only the issue card: the
question as a stranger would put it, with no names, parties or outcome. They
work out an answer on the board, and afterwards we compare it with what the
Capitol actually did.

The background brief for each issue — what the Capitol decided, who was in
power, how the public was consulted, and what has happened since — is
published here after that issue has been run, together with the report on
the day. Until then, only the card is public.
