The Saturday Board

A noticeboard, a lot of coloured sticky notes, and a real decision from the Wisconsin State Capitol, put to whoever walks past the stall at the Dane County Farmers' Market in Madison.

Visitors say what worries them about the question, write down their first idea, file it beside the ideas most like it, and trim the ones they think would make other people's problems worse. By the end of the day the board settles on an answer, and we compare it with what the Capitol actually did.
